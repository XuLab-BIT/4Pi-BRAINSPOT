4Pi-BRAINSPOT integrates 4Pi single-molecule switching nanoscopy, dynamic in-situ coherent PSF retrieval, tissue clearing, light-sheet illumination, and molecular-coordinate analysis for nanoscale 3D imaging in thick mouse brain sections.
